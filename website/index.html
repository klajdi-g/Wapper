A few things before you begin.

Installation & help

  1. Choose a macOS build matching your Mac’s architecture. On your Mac, use Apple menu → About This Mac to identify Apple silicon or Intel.
  2. Compare the downloaded file’s SHA-256 with the value shown here. In Terminal, run shasum -a 256 followed by the downloaded file’s path.
  3. Open the downloaded archive or disk image, then move the app to Applications when applicable. Follow macOS security checks.
  4. If macOS blocks an unsigned or unnotarized build, wait for a Developer ID–signed, notarized release. Do not disable Gatekeeper or remove quarantine protections.
  5. Download a video only when its license permits your use, then open it in the native app. This website cannot apply desktop wallpapers.

For release updates and project information, visit Voltrum (opens in a new tab)↗. The catalog source link is the place to check media rights and attribution.

Creators & licenses

Motion is a window into independently created work, not a claim of ownership. Every catalog entry names its creator, lists its license, and links to its original source.

Read the source’s full license before downloading, editing, sharing, or using a video commercially. A download link does not grant rights beyond that license. Preserve any required attribution.

Preview media is loaded from the catalog’s remote hosts. This site does not bundle third-party proprietary assets or private videos. The abstract artwork and mark on this page are original Voltrum Motion website graphics, not a catalog wallpaper.

Privacy, in plain language

No accounts, analytics scripts, advertising, or tracking cookies are included in this website. Saved favorites stay in this browser’s local storage; they are not synced or sent to us.

The site requests its catalog and release manifest from this host. Preview images contact their remote hosts as you browse; video requests start only when you explicitly open a preview. Those hosts receive your IP address and may have their own privacy policies.

Downloads and source links contact their respective hosts. The website’s host may keep standard access logs. Browser storage can be cleared below; this does not remove downloaded files. This statement covers the website, not the native app.
