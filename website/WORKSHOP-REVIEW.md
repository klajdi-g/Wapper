# Wallpaper Engine source review

Checked October 30, 2026. No Steam Workshop wallpapers were imported or approved
as part of the Aurora-removal update.

## First-party references

- [Wallpaper Engine: editing downloaded wallpapers](https://help.wallpaperengine.io/en/functionality/editingwallpapers.html)
  says republishing can require the original authors' rights and recommends
  contacting them when unsure. It distinguishes video, web, scene, and application
  wallpapers. A downloadable scene package is not the author's editable project.
- [Steam Subscriber Agreement, sections 2 and 6](https://store.steampowered.com/subscriber_agreement/)
  distinguishes a subscription's usage rights from distribution rights. Workshop
  contributions are generally free to subscribers but still subject to the
  agreement and any app-specific terms. A free price is not an asset-level license
  to bundle that contribution in a different app or website.

## Admission checklist

1. Identify an actual wallpaper and its original author, not just an aggregator
   or a repository containing a player/unpacker.
2. Obtain explicit permission or an asset license covering our exact thumbnail,
   video, audio, website, app-bundling, download, and commercial-use scopes.
   Do not treat a player's MIT/GPL license as the license of all its wallpapers.
3. Review music, recognizable characters, and third-party components separately.
4. Record required credits and durable dated evidence for that exact asset.
5. Confirm format compatibility. Motion's native video player does not acquire
   a Wallpaper Engine scene/application runtime merely by importing a file.
6. Add only fully reviewed candidates through the existing shared publication
   policy. The current policy remains paused, with no real IDs approved.
